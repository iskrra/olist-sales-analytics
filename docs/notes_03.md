# Day 3 — Product & Seller Performance Notes

## What I Did
Analysed which product categories drive the most revenue and built a seller performance scorecard combining revenue, reviews, and delivery speed. The seller concentration query is a useful risk-analysis tool because it gives us insight into whether the platform is dependent on a small number of high-revenue sellers.

## Key Findings
- Top category by revenue: Health & Beauty at R$1.23M (~9.5% of total)
- Top 3 categories revenue-wise (Health & Beauty; Watches & Gifts; Bed, Bath & Tableware) account for approximately 26.3% of the total revenue
- Top products by revenue are mostly concentrated within the top-performing categories.
- The top 10 sellers account for ~13% of total revenue, with the top one bringing around 1.7% of the total revenue — the seller base is well-diversified, with no single seller posing significant concentration risk.
- All of the biggest sellers deliver orders on average more than 10 days ahead of time 
- Categories with above-average review scores include books and tools & small appliances; while furniture-related categories, Christmas supplies, and fashion shoes deliver faster than the platform average.

## Interesting Observations
- Avg delivery days vary across sellers - suggests delivery performance is seller-driven, not just geography
- Further category benchmark analysis with cross-referencing could be worth doing, in order to identify categories that excel in both dimensions as well as those that underperform in both (highest priority for improvement).
