# Day 1 Findings

## Dataset Scope
- Dataset spans Sep 2016 to Oct 2018
- Several months incomplete and excluded from trend analysis

## Data Quality
- No major orphaned product/seller/customer references
- Delivery timestamps missing primarily for non-delivered orders

## Business Observations
- Strong Black Friday spike in Nov 2017
- Delivered orders dominate the dataset
- Clean analysis window determined to be Jan 2017 – Aug 2018

## Analytical Decisions
- Future revenue analysis will filter to delivered orders only
- Time-series analysis restricted to complete months
